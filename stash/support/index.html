Getting Pro back on a new device

Stash has no account, so Pro is tied to your Apple ID. In Settings, open the upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases. Apple confirms what you bought and Pro comes back.

Cancelling a subscription

Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Stash. Apple handles billing and refunds; you can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Syncing between iPhone and iPad

Sign in to the same iCloud account on both devices and make sure iCloud is turned on for Stash in iOS Settings. Changes sync automatically when you're online. A small badge at the top of the app tells you when you're offline or iCloud is off.

Pattern PDFs stay on the device where you added them. Add the PDF again on another device if you want it there too.

Counting from the Lock Screen

When you count a row, Stash shows a Live Activity on your Lock Screen with plus and minus buttons, so you can keep counting without unlocking. You can also add the Stash widget to your Home Screen, or say "Add a row in Stash" to Siri.

Keeping a backup

Settings → Backup & restore → Create backup saves your projects, stash, patterns and journal as a file you can keep in Files or send to yourself. Photos and pattern PDFs aren't included, so keep your own copies of those. Restoring a backup adds what's missing and never deletes anything.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Delete All My Data, then type DELETE to confirm. Everything is erased from this device straight away, and from your private iCloud and other devices as soon as they're online. There's no Stash account to close.
