Onward — Support

Write to dmaulikr@gmail.com. A person reads every letter.

Restoring your purchase

Open Settings in Onward and choose Restore purchases. Onward asks Apple, who keeps the record with your Apple ID. One purchase covers every journey, and it can be shared with your family.

Your walk and your words

Everything you write and every day you walk stays on your phone. Onward has no account and no server, so we cannot see it, recover it or move it for you. Your walk does not move to a new phone; before you change phones, you can export it from Settings to keep a copy.

The daily reminder

Onward sends one reminder a day, only if you ask for it. You can change its time, its words, or turn it off in Settings.

If you are struggling

Settings in Onward has a page called Help in a crisis, with numbers for trained people in your region. Onward is not a substitute for care.
